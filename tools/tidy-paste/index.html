Turn a messy list into a spreadsheet

Paste anything — names, emails, phone numbers, a blob copied out of a PDF. Get clean columns you can drop straight into Excel or Google Sheets.

Nothing you paste leaves this page. There is no server, no upload, and no analytics. The whole tool is one HTML file doing the work in your browser — you can disconnect from the internet after the page loads and it still works. That is the point: you can use it on data you would never paste into a chatbot.

What it does

  • Works out how your text is separated even when it is inconsistent — some rows with tabs, some with dashes, some with runs of spaces.
  • Understands proper CSV quoting, so "Smith, Dana" stays in one cell.
  • Can ignore the structure entirely and just pull every email address, phone number or link out of a wall of text.
  • Rename a column by typing in its header. Remove one with the x.
  • “Copy for Excel / Sheets” copies tab-separated text, which pastes into real columns rather than one squashed cell.
